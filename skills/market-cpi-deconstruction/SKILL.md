# Market Intelligence & Competitor Deconstruction

## Overview
Analytical framework for evaluating why a 2D mechanic succeeds in mobile charts (App Store / Google Play), choreographing the first 3 seconds of user acquisition (CPI) creatives, and deconstructing 2–3 market leaders to expose an actionable market gap.

## When to Use
- Executing `/analyze` Stage 2 (Phases 1 and 2) after a concept is approved
- Designing a 3-second visual hook (`0-1s`, `1-2s`, `2-3s`) for hyper-casual or hybrid-casual ad creatives
- Benchmarking 2–3 competitor titles to identify D1/D3 churn friction points

## Core Structure

### 1. 3-Second Visual CPI Hook Choreography
Every approved concept must specify an exact second-by-second ad hook:

| Window | Role | Required Visual Content |
|---|---|---|
| **0.0s – 1.0s** | **Visual Tension / Pattern Interrupt** | High-contrast setup showing immediate danger, asymmetrical clutter, or a tempting multiplier target |
| **1.0s – 2.0s** | **One-Finger Input & Twist** | Clear finger gesture (`Drag`, `Tap`, `Hold`) triggering the core mechanic or a deliberate "near-miss / fail" bait |
| **2.0s – 3.0s** | **ASMR Payoff / Chain Reaction** | Explosive cascade, satisfying sorting alignment, or multiplier shatter that compels the viewer to install |

### 2. Competitor Deconstruction Matrix (2–3 Market Leaders)
For each benchmarked competitor in the sub-genre, deconstruct three mandatory pillars:
1. **Core Psychological & Mechanical Driver:** Which human compulsion (e.g., completionism, trajectory mastery, exponential growth, kinetic destruction) drives D1 engagement?
2. **Friction Points & Churn Risks:** Where do players churn by D3/D7? (e.g., passive waiting loops, repetitive level layouts, unfair RNG spikes, lack of mid-turn agency).
3. **Untapped Market Gap:** The exact mechanical delta our concept introduces to solve the competitors' churn friction.

## Common Mistakes
- **Vague Competitor Praise:** Listing popular games without pinpointing *where* and *why* players churn.
- **Text-Reliant CPI Hooks:** Designing hooks that require reading on-screen captions instead of pure 2D visual motion and contrast.
